## 1. The 5-Point Audit Structure (Delivered as a 2-Page PDF)

When prospecting a company, run this 10-minute check on their current website or app:

### Point 1: Core Web Vitals & Speed Benchmark
- **Tool:** Google PageSpeed Insights & GTmetrix.
- **What to Look For:**
  - Largest Contentful Paint (LCP) > 2.5s (Red flag).
  - Cumulative Layout Shift (CLS) > 0.1 (Unstable visual shifts).
  - Heavy unoptimized imagery or blocking third-party scripts.
- **Client Pitch Snippet:** *"Your mobile homepage takes 4.9s to load on 4G networks. Research by Google proves that 53% of mobile visits are abandoned if pages take longer than 3 seconds to load."*

### Point 2: Mobile UX & Responsive Breakpoints
- **What to Look For:**
  - Horizontal scrolling bugs on mobile devices.
  - Tiny tap targets / overlapping buttons on checkout forms.
  - Missing click-to-call or instant WhatsApp contact triggers.
- **Client Pitch Snippet:** *"On standard iPhone and Android screens, your primary inquiry form has a clipping issue on the submit button, causing potential lead loss."*

### Point 3: Security & Header Hygiene
- **Tool:** `securityheaders.com` or browser network inspector.
- **What to Look For:**
  - Missing Content Security Policy (CSP).
  - Missing `Strict-Transport-Security` (HSTS).
  - Outdated framework version fingerprints exposed in response headers.
- **Client Pitch Snippet:** *"Your server is transmitting outdated version headers, leaving the portal vulnerable to automated scrapers and injection bots."*

### Point 4: Technical SEO & OpenGraph Sharing
- **What to Look For:**
  - Broken preview cards when the site link is shared on WhatsApp or LinkedIn.
  - Missing meta descriptions, missing canonical URLs, or duplicate H1 tags.
- **Client Pitch Snippet:** *"When your sales reps share your website link on WhatsApp, no branded thumbnail or title appears, lowering trust before prospects even click."*

### Point 5: Architecture & Conversion Bottleneck
- **What to Look For:**
  - Too many form fields in lead forms (e.g. 8 fields instead of 3).
  - Slow checkout flow with unnecessary page reloads.
  - Absence of edge caching / CDN.
- **Client Pitch Snippet:** *"By replacing multi-step full-page refreshes with an edge-cached Next.js architecture, you can achieve sub-second navigation and a direct boost in lead conversions."*
