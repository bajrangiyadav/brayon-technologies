## 1. The 3 Core Sales Demos (Day 23 Blueprint)

To close high-ticket clients without friction, BRAYON maintains 3 pre-built demo environments:

### Demo 1: BRAYON CRM (Sales Pipeline & Automation Engine)
- **Target Buyer:** B2B service firms, digital agencies, consultancies, real estate brokers.
- **Key Demo Moments to Show:**
  1. **Interactive Kanban Board:** Dragging a deal from "New Lead" to "Discovery Call" ➔ automated reminder triggers.
  2. **1-Click Proposal Generator:** Clicking "Generate SOW" autofills client parameters into a clean proposal.
  3. **Automated Follow-up Cadence:** Show automated email & WhatsApp reminder schedules.
- **Selling Angle:** *"We can customize this exact high-velocity pipeline for your sales reps in 2 weeks."*

### Demo 2: BRAYON Business (Operations, Multi-Branch ERP & Invoicing)
- **Target Buyer:** Distributors, retail chains, logistics operators, manufacturing SMBs.
- **Key Demo Moments to Show:**
  1. **Multi-Location Inventory Dashboard:** Real-time stock counts across warehouses with low-stock alerts.
  2. **Instant GST Invoicing Engine:** Generating a PDF invoice with QR code in 5 seconds.
  3. **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC):** Showing different UI views for "Branch Cashier" vs "Super Admin / CFO".
- **Selling Angle:** *"Eliminates spreadsheet chaos and saves your operations managers 20+ hours every week."*

### Demo 3: BRAYON AI Assistant (Intelligent Enterprise Workflow & Knowledge Bot)
- **Target Buyer:** E-commerce stores, tech startups, internal support teams.
- **Key Demo Moments to Show:**
  1. **Natural Language Data Query:** Asking *"Show me top 5 pending orders from Mumbai branch"* and getting instant table results.
  2. **Automated Customer Support Resolution:** Bot pulling answers from internal documentation and drafting replies.
- **Selling Angle:** *"Add proprietary AI intelligence directly to your customer support or internal operations."*

## 2. The 20-Minute Diagnostic Discovery Call Script (Day 18 Blueprint)

### Phase 1: Rapport & Framing (0:00 – 3:00)
> *"Hi [Prospect Name], great to connect. The goal of this 20-minute call is simple: to understand how you are currently handling [their core process/tech], identify where the biggest bottlenecks or performance lags are happening, and see if our engineering solutions at BRAYON are a fit. If there's a strong fit, I'll put together a tailored architecture proposal. If not, I'll point you toward the best approach anyway. Fair enough?"*

### Phase 2: Diagnostic Questions (3:00 – 12:00)
*Ask, then mute your microphone and LISTEN without interrupting:*

1. **The Current State:**
   > *"Walk me through how your team currently manages [the order process / the website / customer leads] day to day?"*
2. **The Frustration / Pain Point:**
   > *"What is the single most frustrating bottleneck in that workflow right now?"*
3. **The Financial / Time Impact:**
   > *"How much time is your team losing to this every week? What happens to revenue or customer trust if this isn't solved in the next 3 months?"*
4. **The Ideal Outcome:**
   > *"If we could wave a magic wand and have the ideal software solution running 30 days from now, what does success look like for you?"*
5. **Timeline & Stakeholders:**
   > *"When are you looking to have this live? Who else on your leadership team will be evaluating the architecture proposal?"*

### Phase 3: The BRAYON Alignment & Proof (12:00 – 17:00)
> *"That makes total sense. In fact, that is almost identical to the challenge we solved in our recent [E-Commerce / ERP / CRM] case study. They were losing 20+ hours a week on manual data entry and experiencing slow page loads. We built a custom Next.js & Node.js architecture that cut load times to 0.9s and automated their reconciliation. Let me show you a 60-second preview..."*
*(Show 1 key screen from Demo 1, 2, or 3).*

### Phase 4: Setting Up the Proposal & Closing the Call (17:00 – 20:00)
> *"Based on everything you've shared, we can definitely solve this within a 3 to 4-week sprint. I'm going to draft a clean, 2-page Scope of Work outlining the solution architecture, timeline, and fixed investment. I can have that in your inbox by tomorrow 3 PM. Let's lock in a quick 15-minute review call for Thursday at 4 PM to walk through it. Does Thursday work for you?"*
