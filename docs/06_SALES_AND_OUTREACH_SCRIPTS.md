## 4. The 20-Minute Discovery Call Framework (How to Lead the Call)

Never jump into pitching your code. Act as a diagnostic doctor:

```
[00–05 Min]: Rapport & Framing
[05–15 Min]: Deep Diagnostic (Pain & Cost)
[15–20 Min]: The Solution & Next Step Commitment
```

### Key Diagnostic Questions to Ask:
1. *"Walk me through how your team currently handles [process: e.g. customer orders / inventory tracking / client onboarding]?"*
2. *"Where is the biggest friction or time sink in that process right now?"*
3. *"If this problem remains unfixed over the next 6 months, what does that cost your business in lost revenue or wasted hours?"*
4. *"What does an ideal, seamless system look like for you and your team?"*
5. *"What is your expected timeline to have a working solution in place?"*

### Closing the Discovery Call:
> *"Based on everything you've shared, this is a textbook bottleneck that we've resolved before with custom architecture. I will draft a concise Technical Proposal & SOW outlining the exact solution, milestones, and investment, and have it in your inbox by tomorrow afternoon. What's the best time on [Day after tomorrow] for a 15-minute review?"*

## 5. Strategic Follow-up Cadence (60% of Deals Close in Follow-ups)

- **Day 1:** Send Proposal with Loom/Screen-recording walkthrough.
- **Day 3 (Value Add):** *"Hi [Name], sent over the proposal on Tuesday. Quick question: did you have any questions regarding the milestone breakdown in Section 4?"*
- **Day 7 (Social Proof):** *"Hi [Name], was just thinking about the [specific issue] we discussed. Here is a 1-page case study of how we solved this exact problem for a similar business."*
- **Day 14 (Polite Breakup):** *"Hi [Name], haven't heard back, so I assume this project isn't a priority right now. No worries at all! I'll close out this proposal for now. Wishing [Company Name] continued success!"* (This frequently triggers an immediate response!).
