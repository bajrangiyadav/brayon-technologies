## 3. Brand Identity Guidelines

### Color Palette
- **Primary Navy:** `#0A1128` (Authority, trust, deep corporate stability)
- **Electric / Tech Blue:** `#0066FF` (Innovation, energy, digital acceleration)
- **Secondary Slate Dark:** `#1E293B` (Modern UI backgrounds, structural cards)
- **Accent Cyan:** `#00D2FF` (Gradients, highlight badges, interactive states)
- **Neutral Light:** `#F8FAFC` (Clean surfaces, high contrast readability)
- **Pure White:** `#FFFFFF`

### Typography System
- **Headings:** *Plus Jakarta Sans* or *Inter* (SemiBold / Bold) — crisp, modern tech look.
- **Body Text:** *Inter* (Regular / Medium) — high legibility across all screen densities.
- **Code / Monospace:** *JetBrains Mono* or *Fira Code*

### Logo Guidelines
- **Primary Mark:** Stylized dynamic "B" icon combined with clean geometric typography: **BRAYON** (Bold) + **TECHNOLOGIES** (Subtle slate).
- **Favicon / App Icon:** High-contrast electric blue monogram on dark navy rounded squircle.
