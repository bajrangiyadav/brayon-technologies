## 2. Standard Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA)

### MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT
This Non-Disclosure Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of `[Date]`, by and between:

- **BRAYON Technologies**, with principal leadership under **Bajrangi Yadav** ("Disclosing/Receiving Party"), and
- **`[Client Company Name]`**, with principal place of business at `[Client Address]` ("Disclosing/Receiving Party").

### 1. Purpose
The parties wish to explore a potential business relationship in software development, architecture consulting, and digital transformation ("Purpose"). In connection with this, either party may disclose confidential technical, business, and proprietary information.

### 2. Confidential Information
"Confidential Information" includes, without limitation: source code, software architectures, database schemas, proprietary algorithms, client lists, financial information, product roadmaps, and business processes.

### 3. Non-Disclosure Obligations
Each party agrees:
- To protect Confidential Information with the same degree of care used for its own confidential data (and never less than reasonable care).
- Not to disclose Confidential Information to any third party without prior written consent.
- To use Confidential Information solely for evaluating or executing the agreed project.

### 4. Intellectual Property
Nothing in this Agreement grants either party any rights to the other party's pre-existing intellectual property.

## 3. Master Services Agreement (MSA) Summary Clauses

### Clause 1: Intellectual Property (IP) Ownership
> *"Upon receipt of full and final payment for the deliverables specified in the agreed Statement of Work (SOW), BRAYON Technologies transfers 100% of all worldwide rights, title, copyright, and interest in the custom software code, designs, and documentation developed specifically for the Client under said SOW."*
>
> *(Pre-existing open-source libraries and BRAYON reusable utility modules remain licensed to the Client on a perpetual, non-exclusive, royalty-free basis).*

### Clause 2: Payment Milestone Policy (Strict No "Post-Completion" Rule)
1. **Initial Milestone (30% to 50%):** Invoiced upon SOW execution. Work commences only after funds are credited.
2. **Interim Milestone (30%):** Invoiced upon delivery of Alpha/Beta staging build demonstration.
3. **Completion Milestone (20% to 30%):** Invoiced upon successful User Acceptance Testing (UAT).
4. **Final Deployment:** Production DNS handover and live deployment occur only after settlement of the final invoice.

### Clause 3: Scope Changes & Change Requests (CR)
Any requested feature, integration, or design modification not explicitly documented in the active SOW will be evaluated as a formal **Change Request (CR)**. BRAYON Technologies will submit an estimated timeline impact and cost quote before executing the addition.

### Clause 4: 30-Day Hypercare Warranty
BRAYON Technologies guarantees all custom-written code for **30 calendar days** following production deployment. Any defects or bugs arising from deviation from the agreed specifications will be remediated at zero additional charge.

## 4. Client Welcome & Kickoff Checklist

Within **4 hours** of advance payment clearance, the Founder / Tech Lead sends the official Welcome Email:

- [ ] **Slack / WhatsApp Communication Hub:** Dedicated private channel created for project stakeholders.
- [ ] **Project Management Board:** Trello / Notion / Jira board initialized with sprint epics.
- [ ] **Private GitHub Repository:** Initialized under BRAYON Technologies org with branch protections.
- [ ] **Staging Environment:** Deployed URL (e.g. `clientname-staging.vercel.app`) ready for continuous integration.
- [ ] **Weekly Demo Cadence:** Fixed calendar invite for a 20-minute weekly progress review (every Friday).
